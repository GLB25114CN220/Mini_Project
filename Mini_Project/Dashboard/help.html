Booking Guidelines

  1. Sign in with the admission ID given by the college.
  2. Open Dress Catalog (or the Winter / Summer uniform pages) and choose the item and your size.
  3. Add the items to your Shopping Cart and submit them as one request.
  4. The Dress Management office reviews the request: it becomes Approved (or Rejected) and is then Dispatched when you receive it.
  5. Follow every step under My Orders. Stock is reduced only when a request is dispatched.

Return Policy

  • For a wrong size, a damaged item or a return, send a message through Student Query with your request ID.
  • Please keep items unused and in their original condition until the office confirms the exchange or return.
  • The Dress Management office decides each case and replies to your query.

The college can edit this text in help.html to match its final policy.

FAQs

My admission ID does not work. What should I do?

Contact the Dress Management office. Your ID must be registered by the college before you can sign in. You may type it with or without dashes.

How do I know if my request was approved?

Open My Orders. The status changes from Pending to Approved, Dispatched or Rejected.

Can I change my size after submitting?

Send a message through Student Query with your request ID and the size you need.

Where do I ask other questions?

Use Contact Support (the Student Query form). The office replies there.

Privacy Policy

The system keeps only what is needed to manage uniform requests: your name, roll number, branch, section, year, admission ID and your requests and queries. This data is used by the Dress Management office and is not shared outside the college. Passwords of administrators are stored in hashed form.

Terms of Use

This portal is for students and staff of GL Bajaj Institute of Technology and Management. Use your own admission ID, give correct details in requests and queries, and do not try to access areas meant for administrators. The college may update these terms and the portal as its needs change.
